    Gas Safety and the law

    What a compliant LPG installation looks like, and why the certificate matters.

    1m

    From windows and doors

    Cylinders sit at least 1m horizontally from openings unless an approved fire barrier is built.

    2m

    From drains and gullies

    LPG is heavier than air and sinks, so keep bottles away from drains, pits and basement openings.

    5m

    From electrical sparks

    Keep clear of switchboards, motors, pool pumps, aircon compressors and unsealed switches.

    Why the CoC matters

    Insurance

    An uncertified gas appliance can give your insurer grounds to reject a fire or explosion claim.

    OHS Act

    Permanent gas installations need a valid Certificate of Compliance under the Pressure Equipment Regulations.

    Property transfers

    Conveyancers ask for a gas CoC alongside the electrical and beetle certificates.

    Real testing

    Certification needs a physical pressure drop test, a ventilation check and safe clearances.

    Smell gas right now?

    Close the cylinder valve, open windows, switch off nothing electrical, leave the area and call 010 020 6317.

    Questions, answered

    Why do I legally need a Certificate of Compliance?

    Any permanent gas installation must be certified by a registered gas practitioner. Without a valid CoC your insurance can be affected and you cannot transfer your property.

    Can a 9kg bottle go inside a cupboard under the hob?

    No. Cylinders belong outdoors, upright, in a ventilated spot with the clearances listed on our Safety page.

    How much can a gas geyser save me?

    It depends on your usage. Customers who replace an electric geyser often see a clear drop in their monthly bill. We can estimate it from a photo of your setup.

    Do I need a separate plumber or electrician?

    No. Our team handles the gas, flue and water tie-ins for hob and geyser installs.

    How long does an installation take?

    Most hob installs take a few hours and most geyser installs about half a day, depending on the layout.

    How often is a new CoC needed?

    Whenever the installation is changed, the property is sold, or your insurer asks for an updated certificate.
